ParaArenaConnect

Find. / Match. / Play.

A platform where physically challenged people can find nearby players, accessible sports facilities, coaches, events, and compatible teams based on their abilities and preferences.

00The big idea
“The easiest way for people with different abilities to find someone nearby to play with, at a venue they know is genuinely accessible.”

And the Accessibility Score + SmartMatch + Partner Finder + community-verified accessibility data could make the concept genuinely differentiated.

  • Match on ability and preference, not just location.
  • Score every venue for accessibility, verified by the people who go there.
  • Focus on what someone can play — never on what they can't.

          16Important for the branding

          “What can you play?”

          Rather than

          “What disability do you have?”

          Someone might be a wheelchair user, amputee, visually impaired, or have another physical limitation — but the important thing for the matching engine is what activities they can and want to participate in.

          You can still collect accessibility preferences where necessary, with strong privacy controls.

          17Don't isolate your users

          Inclusive for non-disabled players too

          Allow anyone who wants inclusive or adaptive sport to participate, where appropriate.

          For example, an Inclusive Badminton — 8 players game could include disabled and non-disabled players. That helps avoid creating a platform that unintentionally isolates its users.
